## Added

- On far Overworld destinations, the HUD now also shows the distance in the Nether (1/8). It can be turned off with `netherShortcutHintEnabled`.

## Changed

- The 1.0.0 release marks the settings (`config/xaeronav-client.toml`) and the `/xaeronav` commands as stable.
- Removed 12 internal tuning options from the config file: `heuristicWeight`, `maxExpandedNodes`, `searchHorizontalMargin`, `searchVerticalMargin`, `detailHorizonBlocks`, `recalcIntervalTicks`, `flightHeuristicWeight`, `flightMaxExpandedNodes`, `flightExtendMaxExpandedNodes`, `flightRecalcIntervalTicks`, `flightDeviationThresholdBlocks` and `flightCellBlocks`. Existing config files keep working; the leftover entries are ignored and the defaults always apply.
