## Improved

- The map of the area around you that XaeroNav builds for routing uses about a quarter less memory (at most about 460 MB instead of about 610 MB). Routes are unchanged.

## Fixed

- Long routes in the End no longer turn back toward where you came from each time you reach the tip of an island, and no longer get stuck showing no route at all. Routes now bridge across to the next island toward the destination, including bridges that turn a corner, pass under a small floating island, or cross high above another island.
- In the End, a route can now start from a bridge you built yourself or from the edge of a small island.
- Placing a block a little early where the route shows a placement (for example, before stepping up to where the route climbs by placing a block under your feet) no longer sends you on a detour. The route now climbs onto the block you placed.
- In the Nether, routes no longer build a long bridge along an invisible edge of the search area when a short walk around would be quicker. Some routes were up to about 25% slower than the best route before this fix.
