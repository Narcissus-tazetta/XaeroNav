## Fixed

- Long routes in the End no longer turn back toward where you came from each time you reach the tip of an island, and no longer get stuck showing no route at all. Routes now bridge across to the next island toward the destination, including bridges that turn a corner, pass under a small floating island, or cross high above another island.
- In the End, a route can now start from a bridge you built yourself or from the edge of a small island.
- When a route climbs a step by placing a block under your feet, the placement outline now appears only once you stand at that spot. Placing the block early from the side used to fill the spot you had to stand on, so the route detoured and showed another placement next to it.
