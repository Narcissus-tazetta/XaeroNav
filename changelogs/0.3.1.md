## Improved

- Routes notice a detour sooner and switch to the shorter way, especially in the Nether. The area XaeroNav maps around you for routing now reaches 224 blocks instead of 160, as long as Java has at least 2.5 GB of memory (see Changed).
- Building that map of the area around you is a little faster.
- The game log records more detail about how a route was chosen and what took time, which makes routing reports much easier to investigate.

## Fixed

- In the Nether, the route line could suddenly shrink or disappear while you were following it.
- A route that crosses bridges you are about to place could be thrown away and replaced with a much shorter one.
- A branch that ran into a dead end and came back alongside itself could stay on the route for over a minute.
- Guidance could stall for 10 seconds or more in the Nether while a slower fallback search ran.
- Digging a block the route told you to dig could make the whole route recalculate, often switching direction or ending just ahead of you.
- Lava flowing into a spot you had already dug was not noticed, so the route could lead you into it.
- A destination set on a part of the map that was not loaded yet could end up inside a block, which made routing toward it much worse. It is now moved to a spot you can stand on once that area loads, without clearing the route you are following.
- If building the map of the area around you failed (for example, when memory ran short), it was retried every tick. It now waits 30 seconds and keeps guiding you in the meantime.

## Changed

- XaeroNav needs a Java heap of at least 2 GB, the official launcher's default. Below 2.5 GB it maps a smaller area around you (160 blocks) to leave room for the game.
- On Minecraft 1.21.1, map integration now needs Xaero's World Map 1.46.0 or newer and Xaero's Minimap 26.5.0 or newer, the same as on 1.20.1.
