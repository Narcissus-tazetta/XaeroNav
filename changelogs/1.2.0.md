## Added

- Routes can now use escalators from [Minecraft Transit Railway](https://modrinth.com/mod/minecraft-transit-railway). Running escalators are only taken in the direction they move.
- When a [Minecraft Transit Railway](https://modrinth.com/mod/minecraft-transit-railway) train is faster than walking, XaeroNav suggests it. Accept in chat or with `/xaeronav transit` to be guided to the platform, told where to get off, and guided on from there. It can be turned off with `transitSuggestionsEnabled`.
