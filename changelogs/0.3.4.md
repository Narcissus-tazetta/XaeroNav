## Added

- Minecraft 1.21.3 support, on NeoForge 21.3 or newer, Forge 53.1.2 or newer, and Fabric with Fabric Loader 0.15.11 or newer and Fabric API 0.114.1 or newer.
- Minecraft 1.20.6 support, on NeoForge 20.6 or newer, Forge 50.2.1 or newer, and Fabric with Fabric Loader 0.15.11 or newer and Fabric API 0.100.8 or newer.
- Minecraft 1.20.2 support, on Forge 48 or newer and Fabric with Fabric Loader 0.15.11 or newer and Fabric API 0.91.6 or newer.
- More Minecraft versions on Fabric, using an existing jar: 26.1 and 26.1.1 (the 26.1.2 jar), 1.21.9 (1.21.10), 1.21.6 and 1.21.7 (1.21.8), 1.21 (1.21.1), 1.20.5 (1.20.6) and 1.20.3 (1.20.4). Xaero no longer updates its mods for some of these, so use the versions listed in the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Narcissus-tazetta/XaeroNav#installation). On 1.21 and 1.20.3, use World Map 1.41.2 with Minimap 25.3.2 if you want both.
- New `strictLimits` setting (off by default, also in the settings screen): keeps the limits on bridge length, time underwater, fall damage, risky jumps and blocks carried even when no route fits them, and tells you on the HUD that no way exists within them.

## Improved

- While gliding under open sky in the Overworld or the End, a pale yellow beam marks where to land, with a direction arrow and the distance on the HUD. For an underground destination, it marks where to land before you continue on foot.
- Gliding routes under a roof and in the Nether are found faster and take fewer unnecessary detours.
- Walking routes keep a block away from lava, the void and deadly drops when a way nearby allows it.
- Gliding routes are shorter by default. If you already have a config file, your current value is kept; set `flightHeuristicWeight` to `1.5` to get the new default.

## Fixed

- Routes no longer send you back and forth between the two sides of an unfinished bridge.
- The sky-blue gliding route now starts from the closest point on the route instead of from where you are.
- The HUD no longer says guidance continues when the route ends at a known dead end.
- Clearing navigation, arriving, or taking off now stops the previous route search instead of leaving it running in the background.
- On Forge versions too old for XaeroNav, the game now shows the version it needs instead of crashing at startup: Forge 49.1.10 on 1.20.4, 52.1.2 on 1.21.1, 54.1.5 on 1.21.4 and 55.0.24 on 1.21.5.
