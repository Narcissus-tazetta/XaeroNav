## Added

- Routes by minecart. XaeroNav remembers the rails you have seen in each world and dimension, and when you carry a minecart or one is already sitting on the rails, routes can ride them, even to rails outside your render distance. The HUD tells you where to get on and when to get off.
- Routes on mounts. While riding a horse, donkey, mule, skeleton horse, zombie horse or camel, the route stays on paths your mount can walk and jump, keeps out of water, and tells you where to jump. If the way on needs you to get off, the HUD says where.
- While riding a happy ghast, you get the same flying guidance as with an elytra. While riding a nautilus, routes follow the water.
- A boat-shaped outline marks where to place your boat.
- Stops on the way. Right-click the map or a waypoint to add places to visit before your destination (up to 10), either slotted in where they add the least detour or added to the end. The route on the map and in the world carries on through every stop to the destination.

## Fixed

- Pointing at a forest or a huge mushroom on the map no longer makes you climb the tree: the destination is the ground at its foot.
- Long Nether routes take fewer detours around large lava seas. In places Xaero's map has not covered yet, the route can still change course as the map fills in.
- Standing on soul sand and similar blocks no longer makes the route start below the floor, which kept the route line from appearing at your feet and made it recalculate over and over.
