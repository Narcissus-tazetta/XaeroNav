## Improved

- The map of the area around you that XaeroNav builds for routing uses about 40% less memory (at most about 270 MB instead of about 460 MB) and updates faster. Routes are unchanged.
- Walking lines on flat ground are drawn as straight lines instead of grid-shaped zigzags, so following them is shorter.
- When a shorter way is found as you get close to the destination on a long trip, the route now switches to it more readily.

## Fixed

- Starting deep underground no longer sends you on a long detour at depth before climbing up; the route now uses a nearby way up when there is one.
- Underwater, the route no longer has you dig through blocks as if you were on land; digging there is much slower, so it now prefers swimming around or surfacing first.
- Heading to a far destination from a place your world map has not recorded yet (such as a cave right after teleporting) no longer leaves the route stuck, going back and forth, or being thrown away; it now heads toward the destination.
- In the End, routes to a far destination no longer stop at the edge of an island when the next island is beyond the loaded area; the route now builds the bridge toward it.
- Near some destinations that are hard to reach, the route no longer keeps flipping between two directions and sending you back and forth along the same stretch.
- Destinations deep underground close by, such as a cave below the seabed or below a mountain, are now reached instead of the route turning back and forth near them.
