## Improved

- Routing is now built on a navigation graph of the area around you. Routes appear in places where none could be found before, and take noticeably fewer detours — in the Overworld, the Nether and the End.
- Pathfinding is about 2.6–2.9× faster.
- The HUD now tells you what to do next — mine, place a block, jump, climb — instead of only where to turn.
- Dangerous stretches are drawn as a dashed line as well as coloured, so they can be told apart without relying on colour.
- Routes are reviewed as the map fills in, so a route planned from a partly loaded map gets straightened out while you walk.

## Fixed

- In the Nether, the route could send you around in circles, walking back and forth instead of heading for the destination.
- In the Nether, the route could suddenly switch to a long detour partway through a journey.
- Routes across wide lava seas could fail to appear, or head into the lava instead of around it.
- A route that already reached the destination could be thrown away and recomputed when a short reconnection was all that was needed.
- Routes could fail to appear on the outer End islands.
- The route line did not extend from under your feet right after `/xaeronav goto`.
- The route could recalculate over and over at the same spot.
- Moves that are not actually possible are no longer used: jumping while holding a vine or ladder, placing a bridge while holding one, and jumping out of a cobweb. Falling costs after grabbing a vine now match real falling speed.
- The HUD kept showing warnings (danger, boat, block placement) for parts of the route you had already passed.
- The HUD showed "arriving soon" and the remaining distance for intermediate waypoints instead of only the final destination.
- A corrupted config file is now recovered instead of failing, and custom values are no longer lost when toggling settings.

## Known issues

- In the Nether, routing can take several seconds to appear and stays busy while you walk.
- Around large lava seas, the route can still lead away from the destination for a while before turning back.
