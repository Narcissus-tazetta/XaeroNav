## Added

- New `routingMode` setting, also at the top of the settings screen: `QUALITY` (default), `BALANCED` or `LIGHT`. `BALANCED` uses much less CPU while walking with nearly the same routes, and `LIGHT` uses the least, on a single core, at the cost of a few more small detours.

## Improved

- The first route to a far destination appears sooner.
- Long Nether routes take fewer detours: XaeroNav no longer mistakes a cliff on the map for an easy way up when choosing which way to go.
